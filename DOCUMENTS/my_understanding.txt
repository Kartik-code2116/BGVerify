🔍 What is BGVerify.ai?
BGVerify.ai is an AI-powered Background Verification platform — think of it like a smart, automated system that companies use to verify whether a job candidate is telling the truth about their past.

🎯 Simple Real-World Example:
A company (say, TCS) wants to hire Rahul. Before giving him the job, they need to confirm:

Did he really work at Infosys from 2018–2021?
Does he actually have an MBA from Pune University?
Does he have any criminal record?
Instead of doing this manually (which takes weeks), BGVerify does it automatically using AI + bots — within hours.

🤖 What Happens Under the Hood (AI Engine)
HR submits candidate → AI bot (Playwright) launches
        ↓
Bot visits employer portals, university websites, govt databases
        ↓
Fills in candidate details, captures screenshots & PDFs
        ↓
OCR extracts text from documents
        ↓
AI (LLM) compares:  "What HR said"  vs  "What website shows"
        ↓
Result:  ✅ Match / ❌ No Match / ⚠️ Needs Manual Review
        ↓
HR gets final audit-ready report
