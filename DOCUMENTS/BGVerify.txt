BGVerify.ai – AI-Powered Background Verification SaaS

Vision

A global SaaS platform where organizations purchase BGCoins (1 BGCoin = 1 background verification) and submit candidate verification requests. AI agents powered by Playwright automate verification by visiting employer portals, government websites, educational institutions, and third-party databases, collecting evidence and generating an audit-ready report.

Future Roadmap

This architecture supports a scalable, enterprise-grade background verification platform where organizations purchase BGCoins, submit verification jobs, Playwright-based automation performs authorized checks, AI validates collected evidence, and clients receive real-time status updates, notifications, and downloadable verification reports.
